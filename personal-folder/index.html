About Him

Brandson Sanderson was born in 1975 Lincoln, Nebraska. As a child he didn't enjoy reading books until he stumbled upon a Fantasy series called Dragonsbane by Barbara Hambly. Through reading this book, he learned that he loved books and even tried creating his own stories. However, since his parents wanted him to have a stable job, when he grew older and entered college at BYU, he majored in biochemistry to become a doctor and earn a solid income. However, after he served a mission in the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, he just felt that biochemstry was not right for him and so he went back to writing, much to the dismay and worry of his parents. At first, he didn't hit it off with his books, but after consistently writing a lot of books, he got better and started to attract a large audience. Now, he has sold over 50 million books worldwide with various different language translations. He is known for his longer than average books and for producing a lot of work, having published well over 70 books.
